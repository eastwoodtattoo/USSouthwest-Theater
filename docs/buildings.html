Falcon BMS · US Southwest theater: buildings preview

Work in progress, 25 November 2026; not released yet. Move the mouse (or a finger) across a picture to wipe between before, left of the line, and after.

  • Every generated building gets a roof shape instead of a flat lid: A-frame, hip or single slope.
  • Roofs are drawn dark. The bright roofs flickered at long range.
  • Chimneys on houses, air-conditioning units on commercial roofs.
  • Footprints, heights and wall types come from real map and survey data. Which roof shape a single building gets is a rule, not a record.

These are preview renders of the building models over the theater's own aerial imagery, not in-game screenshots.
