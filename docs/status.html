The big one: every airfield rebuilt

The airfields of the theater were taken apart and rebuilt from real data, one layer after another. This is the largest piece of work in the project so far.

Nellis AFB, runway 03L: FAA paint, taxi and hold lines, lights and signs on the aerial photo. Drawn from the model geometry, not a screenshot.

runways
1,079 runways at 724 airfields checked one by oneAgainst FAA survey data, the mapped centreline and our own aerial photo. Corrected at 174 airfields, 37 renumbered, 12 closed runways removed.
pavement
Real-layout pavement at 724 airfields, sharp painted pavement at 708Each airfield's own runways, taxiways and aprons from the aerial photo, with FAA runway markings, taxi lines and hold lines up close.
lights
Runway and taxiway lights at 615 airfieldsEdge, threshold and end lights on the real runway ends; taxiway lights on the real taxiways.
taxi + parking
Real taxi routes and 12,081 parking spots at 780 airfieldsTaxi routes follow the mapped taxiways with their names; every parked aircraft faces its taxi lane.
signs
13,472 sign arrays at 708 airfieldsRunway holding, taxiway location and direction signs at every named intersection, readable from both sides.
radio + approach
Tower, ground and approach data for every airfieldReal frequencies and ILS where published, approach patterns built on the corrected runways.

In release 1.0.3: the real-layout pavement, lights, radio data and painted pavement at 524 airfields. Everything else in this list is built and being flown for the next update.
